Geometry and resource accounting

Equal clock dimensions do not identify the cyclic step with this Hamiltonian. Identity padding changes the address count, spectrum and variance. It changes no terminal payload and supplies no extra donor.

03

The native comparison is now testable

Fix a native complex, chain basis, positive metric and complete retained environment. Supply a source-defined isometry E for the clock, work, memory and donor bank. Then compute the operator residual norm(HnativeE − EHprogramme); propagation discrepancy is bounded by |t| times that residual.

The full even clock with length at least four cannot embed exactly in a unit-metric single tetrahedron or identical copies: it has multiple positive frequencies, while that operator has only frequency 2. Other weighted or glued source classes need their own admission proof.

At κt=π the complete closed programme returns projectively, including its outgoing records. Protected export requires a separately supplied capture/isolation mechanism. Human review, physical calibration and native acquisition remain open.
