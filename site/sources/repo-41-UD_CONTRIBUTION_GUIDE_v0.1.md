# Contribution to Unified Dynamics

Public contribution guide · 13 October 2026

Mathematicians, scientists, independent peer reviewers and research software contributors are invited to volunteer on a bounded task. Contributions may include checking a proof, finding a counterexample, independently reproducing a calculation, reviewing a physical correspondence, improving code or clarifying a manuscript.

## Starter tasks

1. Review a declared A/B/C spectrum or frozen-mask continuity argument in UD_REVIEW_REPLAY_v0.1.zip.
2. Audit the receipt thermal-gap frontier: exact error gates, corrected write/storage timing or the physical-scale non-identifiability proof.
3. Recover an exact paper and source equation for a proposed correspondence, including assumptions and units.
4. Submit a small reproducible correction to code, notation or a source citation.

## Volunteer review

Specify the artifact and version, hypotheses, scope and proposed deliverable. A review should state what was checked, describe discrepancies or missing proof steps, record reproduction commands when applicable, and distinguish established results from candidate interpretations.

Independent reviews and constructor checks retain separate status. Favorable and unfavorable findings are both useful. Reviewer attribution and publication of the review summary are agreed with the reviewer. A review of one artifact does not endorse the whole framework.

## How to start

- Open a contribution offer at https://github.com/ud-tetra/UD/issues/new.
- Include relevant background, task or artifact of interest, proposed scope, availability and preferred acknowledgment.
- Agree a bounded review or coordinated task before beginning it.
- Return proof notes, code, a result receipt or a review report with assumptions and limitations.
- Use issues and focused pull requests for corrections; preserve original evidence and record the change.

Attribution, acknowledgments and any coauthorship are agreed according to the contribution. Referrals to suitable researchers and reviewers are welcome.

## Practical support

Suitable computing equipment, compute access, research hosting and support for a separately agreed independent review are additional contribution options. The historical 3 October 2026 planning brief estimated $6,500 for a laptop package and $4,000 for a paid mathematical review. These are preliminary USD estimates, subject to quotations and agreed terms; in-kind resources reduce the corresponding cash need.

No payments are collected on the site. Financial and paid-review arrangements require agreed terms; no charitable status or tax-deductibility is represented. Reviewer payment is for careful work regardless of conclusion.
