# Native action identifiability from monomial anchors v0.1

Let u=(B0,a0,tau0) range over all positive triples, expressed numerically in fixed external units. Suppose an independently specified same-carrier map supplies exact positive anchors y_j=k_j B0^(p_j) a0^(q_j) tau0^(r_j), with known positive coefficients k_j independent of the target action normalization. Set W_j=(p_j,q_j,r_j) and w_A=(1,2,-1). This assumes a monomial map with no unrecorded constraints or unknown calibration coefficients; it is not inferred merely from the dimensions of an apparatus observable.

**Theorem.** A0=B0*a0^2/tau0 is constant on every nonempty anchor fiber if and only if w_A lies in row(W). Equivalently rank(W)=rank([W;w_A]). If c^T W=w_A, then A0=product_j (y_j/k_j)^(c_j). If the criterion fails, there exists v in ker(W) with w_A dot v nonzero, and u_i(s)=u_i(0)*exp(s*v_i) preserves all measured anchors while changing A0 by exp(s*w_A dot v). Thus native dimensionless observations alone, which supply no independent dimensional anchors, cannot determine eta_A=A0/hbar for a fixed external hbar.

**Proof.** Write z=log(u), and b_j=log(y_j/k_j). The anchor fiber is Wz=b. If w_A=c^T W, then log(A0)=w_A z=c^T b, constant on the fiber. If w_A is not in row(W), the orthogonal-complement identity row(W)=(ker W)^perp supplies v with Wv=0 and w_A v nonzero. The full line z+s*v remains feasible and changes log(A0). Exponentiation gives the stated positive family and product formula. This is an identifiability result within the declared monomial family, not evidence that the physical carrier obeys that family.

**Bounded-error corollary.** For the fixed estimator log(Ahat)=c^T bhat, if |bhat_j-b_j| <= epsilon_j, then |log(Ahat/A0)| <= sum_j |c_j| epsilon_j. Errors in k_j must be included in b_j. These are deterministic bounds; confidence coverage must come from measurement certificates. Correlations do not invalidate the triangle bound. Choosing c to minimize this bound is permitted before outcomes under the declared uncertainty budget; it must not fit the predicted eta_A.

**Examples.** Length and time anchors leave the mass scaling free and do not identify action. Independent native energy-unit E0=B0*a0^2/tau0^2 and time-unit tau0 anchors identify A0=E0*tau0. A valid direct A0 anchor suffices with rank one; all three base units are sufficient but unnecessary. In every case the apparatus-to-native relation and known coefficients are additional premises. Measured circuit energy times time cannot simply be relabeled E0*tau0.
