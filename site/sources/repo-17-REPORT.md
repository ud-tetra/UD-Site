## Required independent event record

For a prospective same-carrier test, the generator or observation must supply, **before outcome exposure**, an ordered pre-event \(K4/\Theta\) state, dimensionless native interval, \(c_0,c_1,c_2\) or already auditable signed \(G_p^{S\pm}\), pair integrity, burden lane, eligibility and prior-event boundary. The post-event \(\theta\) or primitive closure/transport receipts must be produced by an independently specified process or measurement whose code does not call the candidate sign or address selector. Record the full no-event exposure windows as well as switches. For six-edge claims also supply typed local edge-map \(U_e\) and closure requirement/receipt states; \(C_1\) relative frame is a different operator.

Freeze generator version/hash, state domain, timing, exclusions, baselines, predicted branch (aligned and anti-aligned), and acceptance rule before revealing independent outcomes. The primary discriminating stratum is nonzero signed source with one-pair \(\Delta w_H\ne0\); fixed-Hamming swaps and structural nulls are separately reported controls. A synthetic independent mechanism may only test mathematical model compatibility within its stated assumptions, not establish physical correspondence.
