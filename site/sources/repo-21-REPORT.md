\[
U_{n+1}=R_nK_nU_n,\qquad
Q_n=I-U_n^TU_n,\qquad T_n=\mathbf1[Q_n=0].
\]

The exact algebra gives

\[
\boxed{Q_{n+1}-Q_n=U_n^T(I-K_n^TK_n)U_n\succeq0.}
\]

Consequently \(T_n=0\Rightarrow T_{n+1}=0\), because a nonzero positive semidefinite \(Q_n\) cannot decrease to zero. If \(T_n=1\), square \(U_n\) is invertible and

\[
\boxed{T_{n+1}=\mathbf1[K_n^TK_n=I].}
\]
