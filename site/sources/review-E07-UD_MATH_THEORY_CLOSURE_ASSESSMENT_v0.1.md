# UD mathematics and theory closure assessment v0.1

**11 October 2026 UTC.** Assessment, not a theorem or promotion receipt. Physical promotion: **0**. Constructor review only. The cited main-branch snapshot was commit `904d9902f60b121acb91e8036e837bff18aa3d9e`; subsequent changes require a new reconciliation.

## Decision

**OPEN — UD is not yet at “math and theory closed; only physical experiments remain.”** It has a substantial exact, reproducible *conditional* mathematical core. The laws determining which event occurs, when it occurs, and how some typed factors and open-system sources update are still not specified or uniquely selected. A conditional pushforward of a supplied event is not an autonomous prediction of that event.

There are two different possible closure targets:

1. **Axiomatic model closure:** choose a complete, internally consistent dynamics on a declared carrier, prove its scoped properties, and freeze an observable/measurement dictionary and prospective predictions. Its axioms need not be uniquely forced by symmetry. After that, experimental correspondence can remain the open phase.
2. **Unique physical unification:** derive the actual laws and constants of nature, quantum matter/gauge content, gravity and cosmology from UD primitives. Current evidence does not support this stronger closure; selecting plausible axioms does not establish it. Constants that are calibrated by future experiments must be declared as free parameters, not advertised as derived predictions.

**Recommended target:** finish the first target, name its axiom choices and parameter degrees of freedom, and keep the second target OPEN. “Only experiments remain” applies to the *specified model and its declared tests*, not to every historical UD claim.

## Closure acceptance rule

Mark **MATH/THEORY CLOSED (scoped model only)** only when: (a) a canonical versioned axiom list defines typed carriers, full state, initial/boundary data, generator or kernel, source/admission/mask rules and readout on one compatible domain; (b) claimed global or finite-horizon dynamics, conservation/budget, covariance, support, limits and known no-go forks have proof or reproducible exact certificates, with independent reimplementation of central identities; (c) free coefficients and constitutive choices are labeled, identifiability is stated, and legacy incompatible claims are frozen/amended; (d) a measurement dictionary and prospective, target-blind predictions, calibration-only parameters, uncertainties, null models and falsifiers are frozen. An unresolved mathematical existence, selection or consistency premise fails the gate. Independent review is a separate required evidence gate, not a physical experiment.

**Prioritized path:** P1 close G1/G2 on a bounded, complete K4 pair-and-edge model including null windows and a pre-event source; seek countermodels first. P2 join G3 to that model and prove joint-domain evolution/resource bounds. P3 derive or explicitly stipulate G4 with dimensions and projection error, then freeze G5. P4 independent proof/code review and prospective preregistration. Only then run the physical bridge experiments; their outcome may falsify the model. A narrow axiomatic model can meet this gate while the stronger universal-derivation claim remains open.

**Limitations:** This is a source-bound synthesis, not a fresh theorem or exhaustive audit of every later archive file. The missing local `Gap2_FINAL_Finite_Genesis.png` was not inspected. No fresh mathematical replay, independent review, held-out source event or physical experiment is claimed.
