Keep the assumptions visible

The finite-image theorem needs a total decoder on a surjective source domain. The recurrent-orbit theorem instead needs stability of the complete decoded state. An exact-zero positivity gate can fail that stability. Irrational glued frequencies remove an exact common period while retaining approximate returns.

Norm conservation only blocks stock when stock is specifically identified with that conserved total. Local readouts can vary. Integer tokens have no proved identification with chain support, energy or action.

The next route is a declared finite-window joint carrier that accounts for decoder, clock, memory, outgoing records and reset supply. Permanent exact absorbing behavior is not supplied by a finite closed environment.
