The finite six-ratio protocol uses three known preparations, 12 scalar readings at h=1/32 and 20 projected inversion iterations. Under its stated bounded error assumptions, the tracking bound is below 6.189×10−13, hence below 2−40 and the requested 2−39 gate. 06

The parallel-work corrections remain visible.

  • A coframe or a chosen right inverse selects a lift; it cannot recover an arbitrary discarded kernel without additional information.
  • Boundary nilpotency does not impose coframe torsion flatness. A Pachner move needs typed incidence/coframe compatibility, not an automatic flatness-violation claim for an undefined edge value.
  • The supplied Q₈ matrices over F₁₃ can be verified exactly. The claimed embedding 2I into SL(2,F₁₃) cannot hold: 120 does not divide 2184. No unsupported C₁₃ or dihedral branching rule is promoted.
  • Twenty incident tetrahedra do not define a self-similar fractal or a Hausdorff dimension above its embedding dimension.
  • Finite fields, geometric frames, norm conservation, clocks and physical gauge groups require separate definitions and correspondence evidence.

The source audits preserve valid finite arithmetic and counterexamples while separating them from unsupported gauge, curvature and leakage interpretations.
