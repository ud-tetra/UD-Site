The conditional rule that selects both

First require face-local affine readout, normalized and covariant under relabeling, with exterior consistency in higher degrees. That fixes the barycentric chain retraction R. Let Q=I−SR.

H*=RᵀGR+QᵀQ

This follows uniquely if the old sector and retained complement are orthogonal, the coarse norm is calibrated to G, and the complement norm is explicitly calibrated to its unit Euclidean value.

K_U=RᵀD_U R

This follows uniquely for a positive coarse observable calibrated on Sc and blind to kerR. Positivity removes hidden cross terms.

These are declared mathematical premises. They do not follow merely from symmetry or the absence of adjustable parameters. A constitutive or measurement source must justify adoption.
