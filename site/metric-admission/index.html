Source metric admission v0.1 · development

Changing the metric needs a new test.

The sources contain more than the unit-metric carrier. Correctly normalizing their operators reveals three distinct obstructions to the full default clock, including a geometric family with five bands.

These tests allow a common rate and arbitrarily many identical copies, with the same metric and coefficients in every copy. They concern complete generator equality. Native parameter selection and independent human review remain open.

03

Inspect the source-defined geometric candidate

C0173 starts from a stretched tetrahedron with q=r²>0. Rule A uses normalized simplex Gram determinants; Rule B squares them. Both are source-authored candidates. A nonregular geometry can split the spectrum into five bands, so the three-band argument does not apply.

Frequencies use the declared operator's rate unit. The shape controls inspect conditional source formulas; they select no native geometry or clock calibration. The proof covers every q>0, not just these examples.

The exact five-band proof

To match all five positive clock frequencies, their squared targets {1,4,9,16,25} must occupy the five distinct native bands up to one common factor. That factor cancels from the ratio identity. None of the 120 assignments of four distinct targets to U,V,X,Y satisfies U/V+X/Y=2. Degenerate bands fail the frequency count instead.

The minimum rational identity gap 1/144 certifies exact inequality. It is not an operator residual or endpoint-error bound.

04

The next admission frontier

A heterogeneous or coupled carrier, a different source complex, or an explicitly weaker prepared-window/endpoint contract can leave these families. Each route needs a declared operator and complete encoding, with clock preparation, fresh donors, retained outgoing records, readout and full restart resources still accounted for.

Exact algebra and separate constructor-team checks are available below. Human review and physical calibration remain pending. Earlier finite-window and endpoint-alias results retain their own scope.
