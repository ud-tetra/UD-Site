01

A local error interface.

The estimator fits cosine, negative sine and a constant baseline in a fixed 2049-sample Hann-weighted window. Its complex coefficient preserves quadrature phase. Every valid output uses measured samples only, with no whole-record demeaning, extension or taper division.

For independently justified envelope variation L and input uncertainty ε, complex error is bounded in exact arithmetic by d = H₁L + H₀ε. The numerical kernel gives H₀≈2.000000000043 and H₁≈0.000311801908507 seconds. Formal deployment needs numerical enclosures and calibrated source/input bounds.

02

Locality does not imply broadband recovery.

The exact carrier and a 44.65 Hz offset pass. Smooth amplitude/phase modulation, turn-on, narrow pulse and omitted-sideband full-state recovery fail. The pulse relative RMS error is about 0.549; its fast envelope is strongly smoothed.

The explicit transfer law includes both main response and conjugate-image leakage. At a 600 Hz offset the main gain is about 0.7694; at 1000 Hz it is about 0.4637. These sampled responses are not a certified continuous passband. No post-result tuning or transfer correction is applied.
