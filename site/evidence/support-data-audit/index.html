02

Squaring a circuit trace changes the spectral information.

The hash-verified circuit waveform contains ten channels with 100 records each. The descriptive diagnostic uses only record zero of the nine live channels: subtract 200 initial samples, demean, apply a Hann window, and compare fixed high-frequency bands for V and V².

All nine squared-signal peak bins fall within one bin of twice the voltage peak. This illustrates the parity warning. It does not certify primitive recurrence, the native Hodge clock, or QPB1’s unrelated ηA=2/1 prediction.

The notebook’s envelope-energy routine uses a nominal 20 nF capacitor. No independent absolute UD support map, full energy/baseline certificate or timing-skew uncertainty is established here. No imported code is executed.

03

The newer Rydberg archive needs explicit axis handling.

All 27 scientific CSV members parse as finite arrays. The bottom figure-2a time vector contains two identical copies of the 21-entry top vector, while the associated arrays are 31×21. The original data are preserved; any axis collapse needs a documented rule before fitting.

Mean-Z signs are signed expectations, not a field-phase witness. The inspected release does not establish an independent UD support or energy map. The 2026 publication links the public archive, improving on the older preprint’s on-request data statement.
