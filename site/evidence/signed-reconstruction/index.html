01

Retain complex phase before squaring.

Each channel’s fixed carrier comes from its previously explored record zero. Records 1–99 are evaluated without carrier retuning, using a ±5 kHz positive-frequency band. The records vary in drive-frequency metadata; they are not identical repeated trials, and the extension is not blinded or independently preregistered.

The signed finite-DFT analytic representation round-trips all evaluated records with maximum absolute error 1.5544×10⁻¹⁵ V. Demodulation retains the complex envelope. Taking its magnitude or square discards sign and phase.

02

The failed threshold stays frozen.

The gate requires signed RMS residual ≤5% and phase-floor coverage ≥95%. Every record fails the composite test; all interior phase fractions are zero. Median RMS residuals range from 4.26% to 9.86% by channel.

The phase floor uses the maximum complex residual over the entire post-trigger window. Its conservatism can retain endpoint effects despite an interior guard. This run does not identify which contribution dominates, and the gate is not loosened after observing failure.
