01

The apparatus-to-model map.

For each physical flux–charge pair, choose scales Φ₀ and Q₀ with a common product Φ₀Q₀=S₀. If the retained apparatus Hamiltonian is E₀ times the target Hamiltonian, the model clock is τ=tE₀/S₀. Invertible readout gains alone do not establish this canonical structure or dynamics.

The two species can use ordinary canonical hardware pairs. The reversed complex convention supplies the second species sign. The normalized model electric variable is not automatically a laboratory Maxwell field, and its Gauss generator is not yet an identified electric-charge law.

02

One instrumented incidence diamond.

Nodes are 0, 01, 02 and 012. Links follow the two downward paths from face 012 to vertex 0. Their incidence signs are b=(−1,1,−1,−1); the cycle is c=(1,1,−1,−1). The graph has four nodes, four links and one loop.

Record 24 state coordinates plus eight outputs measuring the complex couplings independently. A computed exp(iθ) always has unit modulus; it cannot validate the actual coupling. Record clocks, applied sources, feedback, probe disturbance and joint calibration uncertainty.

For isolated gauge-field calibration, the loop phase satisfies φ″=−4aκ sin φ. Its finite-amplitude period follows an elliptic integral, so a finite-amplitude measurement must use that prediction rather than its linear limit.

The proposed component coefficients are a=1, κ=1/16, μ=0 and g=1, with g=0 for calibration. They are declared component values. The diamond is not an invariant subsystem carrying the full tetrahedral trapping theorem.

04

A concrete proposed measurement rule.

CD-1 proposes 20 independent preparations per condition, randomized and blinded, with model sample interval at most 1/1000 and horizon 8π. Physical time and sample rate are chosen after t₀ calibration. These counts are an initial design, not a power calculation.

Engineering targets are 1/100 in the declared dimensionless state, source, Gauss, coupling and energy decision channels, with channel action mismatch at most 1/1000. Use the exact bounds and prefix rules in the protocol; these targets do not replace the earlier endpoint theorem tolerances.

Supply at least 99% joint coverage for every quantity used in a run’s decision. Missing calibration, unresolved controls, saturation, missing channels or unbounded reconstruction/solver error yields HOLD. A sampled residual does not establish a continuous-time bound without an intersample certificate. Weak trajectory agreement alone does not establish the SDW absolute defect path or energy-work bounds.

The next physical return is an actual Hamiltonian/actuator design, calibrated action and energy scales, attainable error allocation, validated reconstruction and attributable non-constructor review. No experiment has been executed.
