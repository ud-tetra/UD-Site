The omitted sideband has amplitude 0.3. Every method’s full-state relative RMS error is about 0.287, even when its fundamental-only error is small. Recovering a selected carrier is a distinct objective from recovering the full signal.

02

A guard leaves boundary influence.

The explicit DFT projection kernel gives a conditional error bound from outside-sample uncertainty. A unit impulse just outside the measured segment has a nonzero interior effect even after discarding 1024 samples at each end.

For the fixed padded filter, the outside-row absolute sum ranges approximately 1.06749–1.64220 across the interior. Multiplying it by an independently justified uniform continuation-error bound yields a numerical reconstruction budget. No such physical bound is established here.

Padding agreement does not certify the unknown continuation. The Hann method changes the operator and needs its own division-error analysis. Constructor floating-point checks are not formal interval arithmetic.
