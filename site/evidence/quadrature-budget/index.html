01

The source premise is explicit.

The complex envelope has spectral support within ±50 Hz relative to the fixed carrier and complex spectral-measure total variation at most 1 V. True amplitude must be at least 1/2 V wherever phase is reported. Combined input error must be at most 1/2000 V; constant baseline magnitude is at most 3 V.

These are proposed design restrictions. They are stronger than observing a spectral peak or a finite-record RMS bandwidth. The earlier faster-envelope and pulse cases are not silently readmitted.
