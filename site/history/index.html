03

Numbers retain their types.

7/768, 960/7, 1/(9π²), fitted spectral indices and the historical target 238.14 appear in older research. A count, a normalized ratio, a fitted parameter and a measured physical constant are different objects. Numerical agreement cannot supply the missing constitutive law or unit map.

The current ledger distinguishes exact internal objects, conditional results, candidates and frozen uses. The public atlas does not rederive a historical constant by backsolving its desired value.
